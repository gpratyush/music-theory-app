# Credits

## Piano samples

The piano sound uses recordings from the **Salamander Grand Piano V2** by Alexander Holm,
licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

The files in `App/Resources/Piano/` are shortened (5 seconds, faded out), mixed to mono and
re-encoded as AAC from the original recordings. One note is sampled every minor third
(A1 to C7) and the app pitch-shifts to the notes in between.
